# 06.4 — forecasts, funding, investment and value

Explicit sales quantity/price/cost, operating costs, operating working-capital balances, depreciation and capex drive NOPAT and FCFF. Hypothetical operating tax has no loss benefit. Borrowing and debt service are outside FCFF; cash forecasts separately require actual dated receipts and payments.

The daily forecast nets same-day flows and reports the unfunded path, first closing-buffer breach, cumulative requirement and additional staged funding. It does not execute loans or infer intraday liquidity. Opening-buffer shortfall is separately disclosed. Conventional project NPV assumes end-of-period annual flows; dated NPV uses Actual/365 from the stated valuation date with each investment supplied once as a signed flow.

DCF uses supplied currency/period-consistent FCFF and WACC, end-year discounting, sustainable positive final FCFF and next-year growth. WACC must exceed growth. Every sensitivity cell recalculates the terminal value. Enterprise value bridges to equity by deducting stated debt/other claims and adding only stated excess cash. A negative residual is flagged, not presented as a market price or money available to spend. Simple financing terms disclose interest, repayment and pre-money dilution; they do not claim debt and equity are interchangeable.
