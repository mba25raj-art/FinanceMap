The operating working-capital examples explicitly define receivables + inventory − ordinary trade payables. Cash and financing debt are excluded. That definition is not a universally prescribed statement subtotal. Other operating balances and non-cash movements require an expanded reconciliation.

The inventory write-down transfer question makes the consistency rule testable: a sole write-down of 4 creates a loss of 4 and a net inventory decline of 4, with cash zero. Either subtract the raw net movement from profit, or add back the loss and exclude it from inventory movement. Mixing those approaches falsely creates cash +4. No receipt exists.
