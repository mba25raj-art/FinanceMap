## Investment and valuation perimeter

The project assumes forty additional annual orders at price one and assigned unit cost 0.4, operating cost two, depreciation six, hypothetical operating tax 20% and maintenance capex six each year for five years. Additional operating working capital ten is tied up at year-one end and released only at the standalone project's end. Initial investment thirty occurs once at time zero; no disposal proceeds. Annual end-of-project-year FCFF is 2.8/12.8/12.8/12.8/22.8. Discount at supplied 10% in annual periods. A one-year delay shifts every flow, including initial cost, one year and is a comparison assumption, not an automatically committed purchase.

The continuing-company forecast separately starts from actual closing January operating working capital. Base normalized annual drivers are units 100, price one, cost 0.4, operating costs thirty, depreciation twenty and sustaining capex twenty. Closing receivables/inventory/trade payables are 20/50/20 (working capital fifty). Buying adds the project's forty orders, cost two, depreciation/maintenance six and working-capital requirement ten. These planning drivers preserve capacity; they do not claim to be posted future financial statements. The company continues after year five with reinvestment, so its terminal FCFF excludes the standalone liquidation of working capital.

Use matching annual FCFF and supplied WACC, normalized sustainable terminal cash and growth below WACC. Value after the already-funded January decisions; do not subtract the earlier equipment payment again. Add only actual cash above required operating minimum three. Deduct debt plus accrued interest/tax claims once. Ordinary payables are already in operating working capital. No financing tax shield is added to FCFF. The quarter forecast, standalone project NPV and annual company DCF have different horizons/perimeters and are not summed.
