# Finance Atlas learner pilot kit

Status: prepared protocol; **no learners recruited, no consent collected and no observations recorded**. Technical QA records are excluded from this pilot. Target five to ten willing learners with varied finance familiarity. This is a small directional usability study, not a test of broad educational effectiveness.

## Before a session

Use the exact reviewed build and record its commit. The facilitator supplies an approved preview; this document is not evidence of a public release. Keep each participant in a separate fresh browser profile/record. Do not import the builder's technical QA backup. Use participant codes rather than names in the observation sheet.

Record browser/version, device, viewport/orientation, zoom, input method and any assistive tool/version. Include mobile and assistive-tool users when willing participants are available; never invent those checks. Allocate a 60–75 minute booking with breaks; this is a facilitation allowance, not a validated completion-time estimate.

## Read aloud and record the response

“We are checking whether the finance activities and explanations are understandable and usable. Participation is optional, and you can stop at any point. We will note the steps you take, where you ask for help and how you explain the result. This is a test of the product, not a grade for you. May I take written observation notes?”

Record Yes/No and the time. A No ends observation. Do not record audio, video or the screen unless separate, specific consent is obtained. Agree who can review notes and how they will be retained before collecting them. Inviting someone is not evidence of their consent or participation.

## Session sequence and neutral prompts

| Stage | Ask the learner | Observe without coaching |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starting | “Find a way to begin learning why profit and cash can differ.” | Entry choice, prerequisites, search/navigation, hesitation. |
| Baseline mystery | “Complete the cash mystery using whichever support you would normally choose.” | First/latest answers, hints, retry, continuation and explicit Finish. |
| Connected company | “Work through Nila's six decisions. Explain the dates and assumptions behind your choices.” | Recognition versus settlement, cumulative funding, affordability versus NPV, financing service/dilution and conditional valuation. |
| Resume | “Leave the activity and find where to continue.” | Whether the existing record is resumed rather than a new one started; saved choices and explanations. |
| Replay | “Try one different saved decision while keeping the original.” | Whether prior exposure and comparable assumptions are understood. |
| New transfer | “Use the Tamarind facts to submit a complete recommendation. Support is available if you choose it.” | First packet, requested assistance, prior exposure, revisions and explanation; do not coach a first attempt. |
| Close | “What was confusing? What would you check before trusting this result?” | Learner's own mechanism explanation, confidence, hidden prerequisites, terminology and accessibility issues. |

If someone is blocked, first ask “What did you expect to happen?” Record the obstacle. If intervention is necessary, record the exact help and time; do not classify the subsequent result as unassisted. Hints and study opt-in remain legitimate learner choices.

## Facilitator reasoning review — after submission

Review whether the explanation identifies the changed account or driver, the cash date/reserve, the service/dilution trade-off, and a condition that would reverse the decision. Record supporting words, a reviewer and date. A defensible alternative is not wrong merely because it differs from the builder's path. Do not convert wealth, value or a check count into a decision-quality score.

Keep baseline, guided correction and first unfamiliar transfer separate. Compare within-person observations and describe missing or assisted attempts. With five to ten volunteers, do not report causal learning effects or population-level effectiveness.

If a later follow-up is feasible, obtain agreement and record its actual interval. Repeating Tamarind involves prior exposure: describe retention/revision, not a fresh independent-transfer result. No reminder or follow-up invitation has been scheduled or sent.

## Issue triage and release decision

| Priority | Examples | Required action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P0 | Incorrect account/valuation calculation; lost saved record; impossible essential task | Stop affected use, fix and rerun numerical plus affected journey checks. |
| P1 | Inaccessible essential control; repeated misunderstanding caused by feedback; hidden prerequisite | Assign Builder/reviewer, repair and retest before release or explicitly exclude affected scope in a reviewed release decision. |
| P2 | Wording, density or navigation friction that does not block the task | Record evidence and prioritise; retain unresolved items visibly. |
| Later | Decorative changes and optional feature requests | Keep separate from accessibility, accounting and task blockers. |

Before publication, attach actual observations, changes/retests, unresolved checks and a concrete version. The current kit alone does not complete tasks 11.2 or 11.3. Actual screen-reader/device testing and willing learners are still required.
